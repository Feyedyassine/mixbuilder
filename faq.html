mixbuilder

Frequently asked questions

mixbuilder is a free web app that turns your tracks into a DJ set that flows. It analyzes each track's BPM, key, energy and structure, then orders them for harmonic transitions, smooth tempo changes and the energy arc you choose.

Build a set — it's free
Is mixbuilder really free?
Yes, 100% free. You don't need an account to analyze tracks, build a set or export it. An optional account (Google or email) saves your sets across sessions. The code is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.
Are my tracks uploaded anywhere?
No. Analysis runs in your browser, on your own device — your audio files never leave your computer. If you sign in, only derived data (BPM, key, energy) and track titles are stored so your sets sync.
Which audio formats are supported?
MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A and AAC. Add individual files in any modern browser, or a whole folder at once in Chrome or Edge.
How does mixbuilder decide the order?
It scores every possible transition on key compatibility using the Camelot wheel (harmonic mixing), BPM difference, energy flow and bass clashes, then searches for the order that best fits the energy arc you picked. Each transition shows why it works, with a suggested mix point you can preview.
What are the energy arcs?
Warm-up builds steadily from low to high energy. Peak-time stays high throughout. Journey rises to a late peak, about 70% of the way through, then eases down for the close. Flat holds a steady energy level.
Can I change the order or fix a wrong BPM or key?
Yes. Drag tracks to reorder, pin a track as the first or last, bench tracks you don't want, and correct any track's BPM or key. Like all detection software, the analysis can occasionally pick half or double the tempo or a related key — one click fixes it.
Can I use the set in Rekordbox, Serato, Traktor or VirtualDJ?
Export a Rekordbox XML playlist, an M3U8 playlist, or a printable set sheet. M3U8 playlists can be imported by most DJ software, including Serato, Traktor and VirtualDJ. Browsers can't see where your files live, so exports refer to tracks by file name: save the playlist in the same folder as your music, or use your DJ software's relocate option if it can't find a track.
How accurate is the analysis?
mixbuilder uses Essentia, an open-source audio analysis library from the Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, for tempo, key and spectral analysis. It works best on electronic and other beat-driven music.
Does it work on my phone?
It runs in mobile browsers, but it's designed for a computer, where your music library and DJ software live. For the best experience, open mixbuilder.io on your laptop or desktop.
