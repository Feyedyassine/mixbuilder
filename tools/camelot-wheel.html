What is the Camelot wheel?

A DJ-friendly map of the 24 musical keys. Each key gets a number from 1 to 12 and a letter: A for minor, B for major. Neighbouring numbers are a fifth apart, so they share most of their notes.

How to mix in key

From any key you can always go to the same code, one number up or down, or the same number with the other letter. Clockwise lifts the energy; counter-clockwise eases it off.
